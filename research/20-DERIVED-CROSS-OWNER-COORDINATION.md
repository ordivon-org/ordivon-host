# 20 — Derived Cross-owner Coordination

## Status

Persistent purposive coordination remains a real phenomenon after Primitive Host / Generic Coordination owner falsification.

It is represented here as a **derived cross-owner relational view**, not as a substance, universal graph, global canonical state, or owner.

## HDF41 — dependency-relative local compatibility

Persistent coordination requires materially dependent relations to remain locally compatible enough for the relevant composite trajectory to continue. This does not require one global canonical coordination state or shared semantics across all owners.

## HDF42 — maintained viability

Persistence is better reconstructed as maintained viability of the relevant dependency / commitment / continuity configuration through disturbance, drift, repair, handoff, replacement, and change.

Maintained viability can be realized by heterogeneous mechanisms. It does not imply one universal maintenance owner.

## HDF43 — order-relative constituted validity

Constituted standing is relative to an admitted constitutive order/context:

`Valid_O(F)`

Host may preserve evidence or references associated with such standing. The standing itself remains external to Host and owner/order-relative.

## Derived synthesis

A bounded coordination view may therefore combine:

- dependency-relative compatibility;
- maintained viability;
- typed continuity and commitment relations;
- typed authority/evidence dependencies;
- explicit source-owner/currentness boundaries;
- externally grounded constituted standing where relevant.

The view must remain partial, source-scoped, provenance-bearing, revision-aware, conflict-preserving, and capable of representing `UNKNOWN`.

## What this does not admit

This synthesis does not admit:

- Generic Coordination as an independent semantic owner;
- a global Coordination database;
- a global truth graph;
- a universal scheduler;
- consensus as a Host requirement;
- cross-owner authority synthesis;
- Goal composition or semantic objective satisfaction as generic Host responsibilities.
