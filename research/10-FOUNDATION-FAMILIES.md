## Architectural reading

HDF0–HDF39 establish a large set of typed relations needed to reason about persistent coordination. HDF40–HDF43 show that those relations do **not** jointly imply one Primitive Host owner.

Positive relational reality survives owner falsification.

## Family I is not a higher tier

HDF41–HDF43 are not a super-Foundation layer over HDF0–HDF40. They are late reconstruction/falsification results that explain why persistent coordination survives while a universal Host owner does not.
