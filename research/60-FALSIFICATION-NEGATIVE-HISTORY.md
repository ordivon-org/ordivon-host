# 60 — Falsification, Negative Results & Repaired History

Negative results are first-class research assets. They prevent later product growth, naming pressure, or cross-owner repetition from silently recreating hypotheses already destroyed by evidence.

## Primitive / Universal Host — falsified

The following broad interpretations are closed at the current evidence frontier:

- Host as a primitive universal ontological kind;
- Host as one central/global coordinator;
- Host as the owner of all persistent purposive coordination;
- Host as a global canonical coordination-state authority;
- Host as universal maintainer of persistence;
- Host as universal task/workflow/scheduler/execution owner;
- Host as global truth, memory, provenance, authority, governance, validity, or legitimacy oracle.

HDF40 is the destructive closure point for the universal/central Host-native ontology/service boundary. HDF41–HDF43 reconstruct what remains without restoring that owner.

## Universal distributed canonical coordination state — rejected

HDF41 rejects the inference that durable coordination needs one globally canonical state or universal semantic graph. Dependency-relative local compatibility is sufficient as the generic coordination requirement at the current frontier.

## Persistence extremes — rejected

Neither a stable-state / rare-repair picture nor a continuous-explicit-repair universal mechanism is generic enough. HDF42's maintained-viability model permits heterogeneous mechanisms and no universal maintenance owner.

## Host-owned constituted validity — rejected

HDF43 rejects any move from durable evidence or Host admission to constituted standing itself. `Valid_O(F)` is order-relative and remains external to Host.

## Generic Coordination / Coordination Plane — not admitted

The early post-HDF engineering hypothesis proposed a broader Coordination / Coordination Plane with Host as an internal Continuity Core. Subsequent engineering falsifiers did not reproduce a non-bypassable shared semantic responsibility sufficient to justify that independent owner.

High-information negative evidence includes:

- derived view experiments that remained useful only while source-scoped and non-authoritative;
- Harness × Runtime late-divergence cases where repair semantics stayed Harness-owned;
- Finance × Workstation transfer/currentness cases where source-owner plus consumer-local adaptation was sufficient;
- Computing pressure/outcome cases where generic Coordination envelopes added no unique action semantics;
- C1 removal of shared Goal coordination;
- C2 removal of caller-neutral external execution ownership;
- C4 removal of generic capability-policy authority;
- C5 removal of Host-owned Runtime diagnostics/clients;
- C9 removal of generic recovery assessment after no unique consumer value was found.

Current rule:

> Prefer source-owner truth or consumer-local adapters before admitting shared Coordination ownership.

## Repaired product-history interpretation

C8 demonstrated that stale broad prose can survive after implementation has already contracted. The repair therefore distinguishes:

- **historical ontology wording** — evidence of what was once hypothesized;
- **current product boundary** — narrowed Host responsibility;
- **frozen HDF truth** — upstream semantic constraints;
- **compatibility names/types** — migration/stability surfaces, not ownership claims.

This distinction is why old engineering artifacts are retained rather than deleted, while the clean research root presents current authority first.

## Preserve, do not flatten

Do not delete or normalize away:

- HDF0–HDF43 revision history;
- HDF40–HDF43 destructive reconstruction;
- Coordination F1/F2/transfer falsifiers;
- C1–C9 contraction/activation/identity/closeout evidence;
- historical product names or compatibility surfaces when needed to explain migrations.

A future hypothesis must answer the negative evidence rather than merely rename it.
