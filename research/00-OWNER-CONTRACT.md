# 00 — Host Owner Contract

## Current semantic/product boundary

The proper noun **Ordivon Host** names a narrowed product/owner contract. It does not imply a primitive or universal Host substance.

Host owns:

- durable **Host Task** continuity;
- Host Journal/CAS admission, exact identity, reference integrity, and local witness history;
- exact Host Task revision fencing and short lease fencing;
- bounded continuity checkpoint admission and response-loss-safe replay;
- bounded handoff and inspection projections;
- opaque-extension / owner-namespace durability without interpreting foreign owner semantics;
- bounded compatibility surfaces retained for real consumers;
- local operational integrity required to preserve Host-owned state: Doctor, backup/restore, release/deployment integrity.

These are owner-local responsibilities. They do not confer authority over foreign source truth.

## Explicit non-ownership

Do **not** describe Host as:

- a universal Host;
- a global coordination plane;
- the owner of persistent purposive coordination as a whole;
- a global truth owner;
- a global scheduler or workflow engine;
- a Runtime proxy or generic executor;
- a generic cognition/model host;
- a Goal-composition owner;
- a semantic-objective-satisfaction owner;
- a universal memory/provenance owner;
- a generic governance, validity, legitimacy, jurisdiction, capability-policy, or authority oracle.

## Coordination wording

Persistent purposive coordination is **real**, but its surviving explanatory form is derived/cross-owner. Repetition and practical importance do not establish a separate Generic Coordination owner and do not enlarge Host ownership.

## Constituted validity

HDF43 `Valid_O(F)` is external to Host. Host may durably carry or reference evidence that a standing was asserted/admitted under some constitutive order, but Host does not manufacture that standing.

Where constitutive/normative admission, consequence, or transformation is in scope, the natural semantic bridge is **Ordivon Normative** or the relevant source/order owner.

## Product name law

> Compatibility is not ontology.

Stable names such as `ordivon-host`, `ordivon_host`, Host MCP, systemd/state/release paths, and retained compatibility value types are product identities. They do not resurrect the falsified universal Host ontology.
